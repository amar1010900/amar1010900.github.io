About me

AQ

Curious about how life works — and how technology can make it work better.

Alongside the life sciences, I have a deep passion for artificial intelligence and the role it will play in the future of biology and medicine. I sharpen my skills by competing — and placing — in competitions, most recently reaching the 2026 NSMO national finals in biology.
